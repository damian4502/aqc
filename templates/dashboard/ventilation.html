Ventilation (ACH)

Finds falling segments automatically, then fits an exponential clearance curve. The decay constant is an air-change estimate when the parameter behaves like a tracer with no indoor source during the drop — typically CO₂ after people leave. On other parameters it is only a relative clearance rate.

Higher ACH means the excess above the fitted baseline falls faster. Compare rooms on the same parameter.
